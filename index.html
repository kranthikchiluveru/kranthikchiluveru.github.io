How I work

Start with the real need

I sit with the people doing the work, map how it happens today and separate the underlying need from the requested feature.

Write it so nobody guesses

Stories with Gherkin acceptance criteria, edge cases and non-functional targets that engineers can build and QA can test.

Prioritize in the open

Trade-offs on value, risk, dependencies and capacity are visible, so stakeholders know what comes out when something goes in.

Run the program, not just the backlog

Multi-quarter plans, dependencies across teams, steering committee updates and change management, so leaders always know where things stand.

Prove it worked

UAT before every release, then adoption, uptime and feedback afterward to decide what to improve next.

A sample of my requirements

Most of my work lives in Jira, Azure DevOps and Confluence. I write acceptance criteria as Gherkin scenarios (Given, When, Then) so engineers know exactly what to build and QA can turn each scenario straight into a test case, including edge cases and data-driven examples.

Here is the shape of a typical story, written for an illustrative feature rather than a real product.

Sample content for illustration only.

Story: appointment reminder preferencesPriority: high

As a customer, I want to choose how I receive reminders, so that I don't miss a booked visit.

Acceptance criteria (Gherkin)
Feature: Reminder preferences Scenario: Customer chooses a reminder channel Given I have an upcoming booking When I open reminder settings Then I can choose email, SMS, both or none Scenario: SMS requires a verified phone number Given I have not verified my phone number When I select SMS and save Then I am asked to verify my number before the preference is saved Scenario Outline: Reminders follow the saved preference Given my saved preference is "<channel>" When my next reminder is due Then it is sent by "<delivery>" And the change is logged with a timestamp Examples: | channel | delivery | | email | email | | sms | SMS | | both | email and SMS | | none | not sent |
Out of scope

Reminder timing rules (separate story).

QA notes

Test unverified numbers, opted-out users and bookings made less than 24 hours ahead.
